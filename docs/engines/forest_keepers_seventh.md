## Episode pitch (one line)

The Forest Keepers go back to the night-shift factory: with his orb out, Twigs gets real hands with pruning blades
that slide out, then the machines build a brand-new seventh Keeper piece by piece, and everyone cheers when its orb
goes in and it opens its eyes for the first time.

Same rules as the Tune-Up (`forest_keepers_tune_up.md`): orb out = asleep while anything changes, every step done
by a visible machine, nobody walks through a wall at the factory doors.
